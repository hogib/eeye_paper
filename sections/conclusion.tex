\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

electric-eye places image processing between the camera and the pilot of an ROV and
carries the picture across the tether. Its visibility correction steadies a cheap,
model-free enhancement by filtering its parameters over time, at half a millisecond
per frame on a desktop processor. Its frame bus gives other programs the camera's
frame and the pilot's picture from shared memory without letting them delay either.
Its transport sends standard H.264 over RTP, and two changes in how the receiver is
fed and threaded reduced the latency from bus to bus from 70 to 4~ms at
640$\times$480; the delay had been in the receiver, not in the codec or the network.
Optimized paths were held to byte-identical output on three architectures. These
results are from the bench, and the work that remains is on the vehicle and with its
pilots. The source code and release binaries are open.
